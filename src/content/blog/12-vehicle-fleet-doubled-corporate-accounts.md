Northshore Executive Sedan was a 12-vehicle operator running on a stitched stack: a legacy dispatch tool, a separate booking site, a spreadsheet for affiliates, and a CRM no one logged into. Their COO, Daniela Vargas, took over operations in late 2024 and made a single bet: get the entire stack onto one platform.

Eighteen months later, Northshore doubled corporate accounts and added eight vehicles. Here's how.

## Step 1: Stop sending customers to a competitor's app

Before Exaltis, Northshore had no passenger app. Repeat customers were calling — or in the worst case, "just sending a Lyft." Switching on the branded passenger app gave riders live tracking, ETA alerts, and one-tap rebook with card on file. Repeat-booking rate from existing customers jumped 31% in the first 60 days.

## Step 2: Make the corporate experience competitive

Corporate travel managers want one place to book, approve, and report. Northshore had been losing pitches to larger operators on this exact dimension. Online Booking with travel-manager workflows and corporate billing — branded for each account — flipped that. Three corporate contracts that had been "in discussion" for over a year closed in the first quarter on Exaltis.

## Step 3: Operationalize the affiliate network

Northshore had always done some farm-in and farm-out, but it lived in email. The Affiliate Network turned it into in-platform clicks: send a ride to a verified partner in a city they don't cover, take a ride coming the other way, ratings and payment history visible on both sides. Affiliate revenue tripled in nine months.

## Step 4: Dispatcher leverage, not dispatcher count

Daniela was clear on the headcount math: she didn't want to hire two more dispatchers just to grow. With auto-assignment, smart alerts, and the new dispatch board, two existing dispatchers ran the increased volume with no overtime.

## The result

- 100% growth in corporate accounts in 12 months
- 8 additional vehicles
- 3× affiliate revenue
- Zero new dispatch headcount
- Two-weekend migration cutover

"We migrated our entire dispatch operation to Exaltis in two weekends," Daniela said. "Our drivers picked up the app the same day, and our reservations team stopped chasing emails."
