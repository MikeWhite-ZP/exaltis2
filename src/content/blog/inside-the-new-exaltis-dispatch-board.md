We rebuilt the Exaltis dispatch board over the last two months, with input from more than 80 active dispatchers. The headline: it's faster, denser, and reflects how dispatchers actually think — not how software engineers wish they did.

## What's new

### Drag-and-drop, finally first-class

Reservations now flow from an Unassigned queue into Dispatched, En route, POB, and Closed columns. Drag a card onto a chauffeur — or onto a farm-out partner — and the trip lifecycle updates in real time. Bulk drag is supported with a modifier key.

### Live driver pins on the same screen

The map is no longer a separate tab. Driver locations, vehicles, and current trips render on the same screen as the dispatch columns, with synchronized hover state. Hovering a trip card lights up the corresponding chauffeur on the map.

### A trip-detail drawer that respects your time

Click any trip and the drawer slides in with everything in one place: rate, passenger contact, flight info, special instructions, payment status, chauffeur, vehicle, and message history. Two of our beta dispatchers reported saving 90+ seconds per trip just from not switching screens.

### Smart alerts, finally explainable

SLA-risk alerts now tell you _why_: late driver, flight delay, traffic incident, or a chained downstream trip that's at risk if this one slips. You can act on it in two clicks.

## What's next

We're shipping multi-trip routing and a "trip chain" view next quarter. It lets dispatchers visualize a chauffeur's entire day, with gaps highlighted so you can fill the dead minutes with short corporate work.

If you want a guided tour, your account manager can walk you through it — or join one of our [live webinars](/webinars) on the new board.
